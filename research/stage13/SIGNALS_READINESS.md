## Contract

The offline scanner supplies a source-bound fixture snapshot with explicit setup, coverage, context and liquidity. The controller gates it against separately injected authority pins, signer callback, revocation, measured/received/available/expiry UTC and monotonic event brackets. Source and policy fingerprints are signed. Calibration specifies offset, uncertainty, drift and max age; brackets must be causal, within the calibrated estimate and under the policy ceiling. Missing or invalid evidence yields QUALIFICATION_BLOCKED, while a complete negative setup yields NO_QUALIFIED_FIXTURE. On local reopen or pinned restore the canonical packet is re-evaluated at its recorded decision time against current authority pins before external mock effects. Changed/revoked pins block replay. Synthetic identities cannot become real IDs. No new permission mechanism is armed.
