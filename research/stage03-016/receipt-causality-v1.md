## Evidence layers

1. `RECEIVED_BEFORE_DECISION`: a pinned genesis/checkpoint and one collector epoch,
   gap-free sequence, immutable source_event_id/revision, previous digest,
   raw SHA/byte length, actual receipt wall+monotonic times, closed inputs and
   exact decision-input digests. Inputs after the decision, superseded revisions,
   repeated inputs, gaps, reconnect epochs and wall/monotonic jumps fail. A
   previous decision is replayed from its frozen prefix; later corrections do
   not rewrite it. Archive/synthetic replays report REPLAY_ORDER_ONLY, not field
   receipt evidence. The short compatibility chain is a post-capture diagnostic,
   not an independent observation guarantee.
2. `UTC_BOUND_VERIFIED`: an independently admitted authority/calibration document,
   pinned by SHA-256 over its actual fields, explicitly non-synthetic, matching
   collector epoch, reference wall/monotonic pair and calibration validity. At
   decision, base absolute error + elapsed*max_drift_ppm/1e6 + wall/monotonic
   residual must be strictly below1s. Each receipt must also lie inside admitted
   calibration coverage, and candle close must precede receipt by its UTC error
   margin. Negative/nonfinite bounds, expired coverage,
   changed attestation bytes and missing independent pins fail or hold. A claim
   that NTS/TLS authenticated a server does not supply its absolute error.
3. `PUBLISHER_ORIGINAL_PUBLICATION_ATTESTED`: remains MISSING without issuer proof.
   HISTORICAL_PUBLISHER_PIT remains HOLD regardless of layers1/2 or CI results.

Candidate acceptance needs actual new prospective inputs, both layers1/2 and
independent review. A synthetic test double may exercise a trusted-input code
path but is not real UTC evidence. Production trust registries default empty;
never populate them from the collector's own assertion or a test fixture.

The integrated future path additionally requires real collector monotonic
provenance and an independently admitted continuous-chain anchor; it cannot
restart a new zero genesis for every decision. The library does not start any
network source or collector. Recovery gaps require a new admitted epoch and
calibration; they never cause automatic capture retry or historical backfill.
